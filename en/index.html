How we work

Four things we do differently

We do more than we promise. Every month you will know where your child stands, what they have understood and what they have not, and no one falls behind without us noticing.

Parents always know their child's level and progress

We believe real progress is built on communication and transparency. That is why parents are kept informed at all times about their child's level, their progress, the difficulties they face, and the areas that still need work.

A progress report every month

A short document: what was covered, the percentage of the curriculum completed, which chapters need work, and what we do next month. It goes to the parent, not just the student.

Make-up sessions are not lost

A session the student could not attend is rescheduled or made up online. It is not deducted from the package.

Mock exams under real conditions

For students sitting official exams, such as the National Evaluation and the Baccalaureate, with timed conditions and the official grading scheme. That way we know the real situation and the progress, not just an impression.

Frequently asked

What parents ask most often

How many students are in a group?

A maximum of 3 students. Because every one of them has to count.

With us, no one is left as just a spectator in the lesson.

Three students means individual attention, real participation, and time for each of them. And we always keep to that limit.

What happens if a student misses a session?

The session is rescheduled or made up online. It is not deducted from the package and it is not lost.

What is in the monthly report, and who receives it?

A short document: what was covered, the percentage of the curriculum completed, which chapters need work, and what happens next month. It goes to the parent, not just the student. For students sitting the National Evaluation or the Baccalaureate it also includes the mock exam grade, so you see the actual progress, not just an impression.

What does the first session look like?

The first meeting is free and comes with no obligation.

We get to know each other, discover the student's level, their goals and the way they learn best, and see whether there is that natural connection between student and teacher.

Only then do we decide together on the right path for them.

What is taught?

Eighteen subjects, for the National Evaluation, the Baccalaureate and foreign languages, from Romanian and math to Korean, Turkish and Romanian for foreigners, plus five courses outside the curriculum: Excel, accounting, diction, personal development, and financial literacy.

Where are the sessions held, and during what hours?

At Str. Edgar Quinet 10, et. 1, ap. 4, 010018 București. Hours: Monday–Friday, 08:00–21:00. Make-up sessions can also be held online.
